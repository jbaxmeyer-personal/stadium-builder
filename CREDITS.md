# Credits

## Textures and sky — Poly Haven (CC0)

All files in `assets/polyhaven/` are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released
under CC0 (public domain). Downloaded at 1k resolution via the Poly Haven API.

- `rough_concrete` — stands and walls; `concrete_floor_02` — concourse decks
- `concrete_tile_facade` — stand plinths; `square_concrete_pavers` — the forecourt
- `asphalt_01` — plot surface
- `grass_ground` — surrounding land; normal/roughness detail on the pitch
- `corrugated_iron` — roof cladding
- `red_brick_03` — brick facades
- `metal_plate` — steel facades
- `plastered_wall`, `painted_plaster_wall`, `oak_wood_planks`, `large_sandstone_blocks`, `granite_wall`,
  `box_profile_metal_sheet`, `exterior_wall_cladding`, `concrete_panels`, `rectangular_facade_tiles`,
  `rusty_metal_sheet` — wall and roof finishes the player picks for each part of a stand
- `sparse_grass`, `forrest_ground_01`, `rocky_terrain_02` — landscape grass, dirt, fields and rock
- `sky/partly_cloudy_1k.hdr` — Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), the sunny sky
- `sky/farm_field_puresky.hdr` — Farm Field (Pure Sky), the cloudy sky
- `sky/kloofendal_overcast_puresky.hdr` — Kloofendal Overcast (Pure Sky), overcast, rain and snow
- `sky/belfast_sunset_puresky.hdr` — Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky), golden hour

Everything else (stands, seats, fans, flags, roofs, buildings, sound) is generated in code.

## 3D models and sprites — Kenney (CC0)

All files in `assets/kenney/` are by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), released under CC0
(public domain); each kit's `License.txt` is included.

- Game Icons, Game Icons Expansion, Board Game Icons — HUD and menu icons
- Car Kit — cars in the car parks
- Train Kit — trains at the station and the subway
- City Kit (Commercial), City Kit (Suburban) — the town around the ground
- City Kit (Roads) — streetlights and street props
- Nature Kit — trees
- Mini Characters — animated supporters walking around outside
- Particle Pack — smoke and flame sprites for flares
- Kenney Fonts — lettering on stand signs and building signs (assets/kenney/fonts)
- Game Icons — UI icons (zoom, home, gear, trash, save, trophy, ...)

Fans in the stands, seats, stands, roofs, floodlights and boards are generated in code.
