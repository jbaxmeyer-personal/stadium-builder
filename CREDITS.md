# Credits

## Textures and sky — Poly Haven (CC0)

All files in `assets/polyhaven/` are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released
under CC0 (public domain). Downloaded at 1k resolution via the Poly Haven API.

- `concrete_floor_02`, `concrete_wall_003` — stands, walls, concourse paving
- `asphalt_01` — plot surface
- `grass_ground` — surrounding land; normal/roughness detail on the pitch
- `corrugated_iron` — roof cladding
- `red_brick_03` — brick facades
- `metal_plate` — steel facades
- `sky/partly_cloudy_1k.hdr` — Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), daytime sky and lighting

Everything else (stands, seats, fans, flags, roofs, buildings, sound) is generated in code.
