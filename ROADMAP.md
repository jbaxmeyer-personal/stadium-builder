# Stadium Builder roadmap

The goal: a fun, SimCity-style game where you build a cool stadium over time and
watch it (and your club) grow. Decisions matter, but a sensible player always grows.

## Done

- Sim: fanbase that grows every match, demand by stand type, atmosphere, results,
  halftime queues, money, fan mood; seasons that chain forever; seeded and tested (GUT)
- 3D builder: click beside the pitch to build, click next to a stand to extend it,
  add rows, demolish; kiosks and restrooms on the concourse; orbit camera
- Stand types: Terrace, Seated, Premium, Family, Safe Standing
- Corner stands that fill the gaps between sides
- Roof styles per stand: Modern (cantilever), Classic (pitched with gable and pillars), None
- Club identity: name (shown on the main stand), home/trim colours with kit presets,
  per-stand seat colours; the crowd wears your colours
- Crowd fills the seats after each match and bounces with the atmosphere
- Tools: matchday runner, layout comparison, autoplay growth bot

## Next up (in order)

1. **Save / load your game** (layout + club + season) so a stadium survives quitting
2. **Matchday moment**: night matches with floodlights, crowd noise meter, a short
   "matchday" camera sweep and the final score popping up
3. **Taller stands**: upper tiers (a second deck above a stand) and bigger roofs
   as stands get deeper; executive boxes between tiers
4. **Growth milestones**: unlocks as the fanbase grows (premium needs 5,000 fans,
   upper tiers 15,000, ...), with a little celebration when you hit one
5. **More to build around the ground**: club shop, fan zone, car park, ticket office,
   statues and big screens; outside-the-ground decoration
6. **Stadium name and sponsor** (rename your ground, sponsor boards on the roof fascia)
7. **League climb**: simple tiers above the season loop, promotion when you're doing
   well; bigger opponents bring bigger crowds
8. **Sound**: crowd roar scaling with atmosphere, goal cheers

## Later / ideas

- Seat-colour patterns (club name or crest spelled out in seats)
- Weather and seasons (snow, rain)
- Steam workshop-style sharing of stadium layouts
