# Backlog: everything between today and the game we want

Principle: we hand the player pieces and tools, never finished presets. Every piece is
placed, sized and styled by the player; every visual thing has depth and mass (no flat
stickers unless "painted" is a choice), and anything that can glow has a choice of how.
Worked top to bottom; `[x]` = done (verified in screenshots), `[~]` = partly done.
